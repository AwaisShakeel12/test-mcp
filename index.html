About me

A developer with an eye for the details.

I care about the small decisions that make a product feel considered — the spacing, the motion, the way a layout holds together from a phone to a wide desktop.

I'm a full-stack developer who enjoys working where design and engineering meet. My approach is simple: understand the problem, keep the code readable, and polish the experience until it feels natural to use.

I build responsive interfaces with semantic HTML, maintainable CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, and I'm comfortable working across the stack when a project calls for it. I value clear communication, steady iteration, and shipping work that holds up over time.

  • Semantic, accessible markup that works for everyone
  • Scalable CSS architecture and reusable components
  • Thoughtful, purposeful interaction design
  • A performance-first mindset from the first commit
